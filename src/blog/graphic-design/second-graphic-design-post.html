Keep a label near the item it explains and leave more room between separate groups. ---

A caption placed close to an image reads as part of the same group. If the gap grows larger than the space to the next paragraph, that relationship becomes harder to see.

Make relationships visible

Use gaps with a purpose. The space around a label should help connect it to the content it describes, rather than separating the two.

Compare the space within each group with the space between groups. A consistent difference helps readers scan a page without adding a border around every separate piece of content.

Proximity also affects navigation. Keep related links together and separate them from surrounding text, so readers can tell where a group begins and where it ends.
