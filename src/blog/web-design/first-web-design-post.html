Align headings, paragraphs and images where their relationship matters, then check the arrangement at narrower widths. ---

A common left edge gives a section a stable starting point. It lets headings and paragraphs sit together without requiring each element to use the same width or visual weight.

Choose a useful alignment

Check the edges between sections as well as within them. Alignment is easier to follow when related elements share a deliberate starting point.

The theme places the post list and category navigation in separate columns on wider screens. Their shared top edge helps readers understand the layout even though the columns serve different purposes.

When the sidebar stacks below the posts, the reading order stays intact. Review the spacing in that arrangement too; a gap that works between columns may feel different between sections.
